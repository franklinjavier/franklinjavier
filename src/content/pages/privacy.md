This website, franklinjavier.com, is the personal site and blog of Franklin Javier. It is a static website and it is intentionally boring from a privacy perspective: it exists to publish articles, with only aggregate traffic statistics.

## What this site collects

- **No analytics scripts.** Pages load no analytics, tracking or advertising JavaScript. The only traffic numbers I see are the aggregate request counts that Cloudflare, as the host, shows in its dashboard. They come from the server side, with no cookies, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.
- **No cookies.** The site does not set cookies. Your theme preference (light or dark mode) is stored in your browser's `localStorage` and never leaves your device.
- **No accounts or forms.** There is nothing to sign up for, so no personal information is ever requested or stored by this site.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com). As with any web host, Cloudflare's infrastructure may process standard request data (such as IP addresses and user agents) to serve requests and protect against abuse. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details. I do not access or store these logs.
